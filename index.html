Achievements

CAMPOR x BUILD Hackathon 2026

Karlskrona, November 2026. Challenge set by Blue Science Park. About 150 participants in about 40 teams.

Our team won the Blue Science Park challenge with Radar, a tool that helps small companies find funding calls, events and test facilities they would otherwise miss. AI agents read public sources and build a fact-checked profile of each company, then rank opportunities with a 1 to 10 fit score and the reasons behind it.

Under the hood: Python and FastAPI, Next.js, embeddings to match opportunities to profiles, a verifier that checks every quoted fact against its saved source, and a guard against off-topic and prompt-injection requests (17 of 17 test cases), backed by 26 automated tests.
